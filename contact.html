Frequently Addressed Topics

  • How do I delete my synced Supabase account?
    Email us with subject "Account Deletion Request" from your registered email.
  • Can you recover my lost Secret Diary entries?
    No. Secret Diary entries are local-only and never leave your phone.
  • Are Google Drive backups encrypted?
    No. They are stored as raw JSON in your Drive AppData folder.
