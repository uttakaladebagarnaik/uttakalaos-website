5. Data & Backup Responsibilities

UTTAKALA OS is architected to grant users maximal data sovereignty, and with that control comes important user responsibilities:

  • User Responsibility for Backups: While cloud synchronization is supported for tasks, routines, and attendance through Supabase, you are strongly advised to regularly utilize the Google Drive AppData backup feature or keep external records of vital information.
  • Unencrypted JSON Backups: As explicitly disclosed in our Privacy Policy and Security Whitepaper, backup files exported to your Google Drive AppData folder are stored as raw, unencrypted JSON snapshots. You acknowledge and accept that UTTAKALA OS does not apply additional encryption to these files beyond Google's native cloud storage protections.
  • Secret Diary Preservation: Secret Diary entries are strictly local to your Android device SQLite storage. If you clear application data, uninstall the app, or wipe your phone without an offline export, your Secret Diary data cannot be restored by us because it was never uploaded to our servers.
  • No Archival Guarantee: We are not an immutable document archiving or legal compliance service. You should maintain independent copies of legally significant certificates, receipts, or contracts.
