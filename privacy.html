CRITICAL SUMMARY OF TECHNICAL REALITIES
  • Not all data is local-only: We utilize Supabase for user authentication and cross-device synchronization of habits, daily tasks, academic schedules, and analytics.
  • Google Drive AppData backups are NOT encrypted: Backups created in your Google Drive AppData folder are structured as raw, unencrypted JSON snapshots.
  • Secret Diary IS strictly local-only: Secret Diary entries remain in private client SQLite storage on your Android device. They are NEVER sent to Supabase or exported to Google Drive.

1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy describes the policies and procedures of UTTAKALA OS ("UTTAKALA OS", "we", "our", or "the Platform") regarding the collection, use, synchronization, and disclosure of information when you access or use the UTTAKALA OS application and this website.

UTTAKALA OS is an autonomous personal productivity and life-management operating system engineered to help individuals organize routines, attendance, tasks, records, and personal development. We are committed to absolute honesty regarding data architecture, boundaries, and security.

By creating an account or using the Platform, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Account Authentication & Supabase Synchronization

To provide persistent access to your personal command center across sessions and devices, UTTAKALA OS integrates with Supabase (an open-source cloud backend and PostgreSQL database platform):

  • Account Credentials: When you register or sign in, your email address, unique authentication identifier, and password hash (or third-party OAuth token handled via Supabase Auth) are securely processed by Supabase. We do not store or transmit plaintext passwords.
  • Synchronized Application Data: Depending on your account configuration, data including your daily routine checklists, attendance logs, tasks, timetable schedules, and focus metrics are transmitted to and stored within managed Supabase database tables associated with your user ID.
  • Row Level Security: Database queries are restricted by PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) policies, ensuring authenticated users can strictly query and mutate only rows where auth.uid() = user_id.
  • Session Management: Authentication tokens are stored securely in your client device session to maintain your logged-in state over TLS 1.3 encrypted connections.

3. Google Account Authorization & Google Drive AppData

UTTAKALA OS provides an optional snapshot backup and recovery feature powered by Google Drive. Understanding how this integration operates is essential:

3.1 Explicit User Authorization

UTTAKALA OS does not access your Google Account without your explicit, voluntary authorization through the standard Google OAuth consent dialog. You can freely choose whether or not to connect your Google account.

3.2 Limited AppData Scope

When authorized, the Platform requests access strictly to Google Drive's dedicated Application Data folder (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata).

  • The Application Data folder is a hidden directory created by Google Drive specifically for UTTAKALA OS.
  • UTTAKALA OS cannot view, access, modify, or delete your personal Google Docs, Sheets, Photos, or general files stored in your primary Google Drive.
  • UTTAKALA OS only creates, reads, and updates backup snapshot files located directly within its own designated AppData container.

3.3 Clear Notice: Google Drive Backups are Unencrypted JSON

Important Technical Disclosure: Backup files created in Google Drive are formatted as structured JSON (JavaScript Object Notation). These backup files are NOT encrypted by UTTAKALA OS prior to upload. While Google maintains transport-level encryption (HTTPS/TLS) and server-side encryption at rest across Google Cloud infrastructure, the JSON content itself is stored in plaintext representation within your Google Drive AppData directory. You should be aware of this when choosing what data to include in routine backups.

4. Secret Diary: Strictly Local-Only Storage

To honor deep personal sovereignty, UTTAKALA OS implements an architectural quarantine for the Secret Diary feature:

  • Local Sandbox Only: Diary entries, private journal notes, and diary-specific settings are stored exclusively in your local Android Room/SQLite database on your physical device.
  • Excluded from Supabase Sync: Secret Diary text is never transmitted to our Supabase database or backend synchronization servers.
  • Excluded from Google Drive Backups: The backup routines that generate JSON snapshots for Google Drive AppData explicitly filter out and omit Secret Diary entries.
  • Device Dependence: Because diary entries exist solely on your local device, clearing your application storage or deleting local app data without an offline manual export will permanently erase local diary entries.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

UTTAKALA OS relies on select verified infrastructure providers to operate the Platform:

  • Supabase Inc.: For user authentication, database management, and cloud data synchronization over TLS 1.3. For more details, consult Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • Google LLC / Google Cloud: For optional user-authorized Google Drive AppData backups. The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • GitHub Pages / CDN Hosting: This official website is delivered via GitHub Pages. Standard server connection logs (such as visitor IP addresses) may be collected by GitHub for infrastructure integrity and DDoS prevention. Consult the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to marketing brokers, third-party advertising networks, or telemetry aggregators.

7. Data Security & Technical Limitations

We employ modern industry transport security (HTTPS/TLS) across all website and application interactions. Database access via Supabase is governed by row-level security policies (RLS) ensuring that users may only read and write data mapped directly to their authenticated account identifier.

Realistic Security Limitations: No method of electronic transmission over the internet or method of digital storage is 100% impenetrable. We do not claim end-to-end zero-knowledge encryption, nor do we falsely claim military-grade security certifications (such as SOC2, FedRAMP, or ISO 27001). We urge users to use strong, unique passwords and maintain security on their personal physical devices.

8. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your synchronized application data on Supabase for as long as your account remains active.

  • Account Deletion: If you wish to delete your account and associated database records from Supabase, you may submit a request using the contact email below. Upon request verification, your account profile and database records will be deleted from active production tables within 30 days.
  • Drive Backup Removal: Because Google Drive AppData backups reside within your personal Google account storage, you may directly revoke access or delete application backup files at any time via your Google Account Settings (→ Security → Third-party apps with account access).
  • Local Data Removal: Clearing your Android application storage or uninstalling the app will immediately delete local cache, local offline databases, and your Secret Diary entries.
