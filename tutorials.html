WRITTEN FIELD MANUAL

Step-by-Step Operator Instructions

Quick reference guides to help you make the most of your daily workflow inside UTTAKALA OS.

1. Daily Morning Initialization (5-Minute Routine)

The secret to sustained focus is establishing a consistent morning orientation cadence before reacting to incoming emails or chat notifications:

  1. Launch Mission Command: Open the dashboard and observe your active habit completion matrix. Note your current streak score.
  2. Set Your Prime Focus: Enter one high-leverage objective into the Daily Focus banner. All secondary tasks support this primary milestone.
  3. Audit Daily Tasks: Review scheduled action items. If any task is overly complex or vague, use the AI Task Slicer to break it down into atomic 15-minute sub-steps.
  4. Engage Focus Timer: Begin your first uninterrupted 50-minute deep work block with phone notifications placed out of sight.

2. Combating Digital Compulsion with the Dopamine Urge Breaker

When working at a screen, your brain inevitably experiences momentary boredom or impulses to open social media, short-video feeds, or shopping tabs.

  • Immediate Trigger: As soon as you sense the urge to divert your attention, navigate to the Urge Breaker in UTTAKALA OS.
  • 10-Second Breath Lock: The tool initiates a rhythmic 10-second guided inhale/exhale sequence while visually pausing all stimuli.
  • Dopamine Reset: In 90% of instances, taking 10 mindful seconds dissipates the automatic dopamine impulse, allowing you to return to productive flow without losing momentum.

3. Managing Academic Attendance & Backlog Clearances

College courses often penalize students whose attendance falls below strict thresholds (such as 75% or 80%). The Academic Vault automates this tracking:

  • Define Subjects & Targets: Input each registered course along with its mandatory attendance ratio.
  • Real-Time Margin Calculator: The vault dynamically calculates how many upcoming lectures you must attend to maintain clearance, or how many sessions you can safely miss in case of health emergencies.
  • Backlog Task Tracking: Keep a dedicated registry of backlogs, assignment submission deadlines, and lab certification sign-offs.

4. Data Isolation & Google Drive AppData Sync Hygiene

Understanding where your data lives ensures you never lose important records while safeguarding personal thoughts:

  • Cloud Backups: Use the Google Drive AppData backup feature weekly. Remember: these files are readable JSON placed inside your hidden Drive app folder. Do not store plain text passwords in routine notes.
  • Local Secret Diary: Your Secret Diary text is NEVER synced to cloud databases. If you plan to clear your browser data or switch devices, export your reflections manually to a safe personal offline file.
