FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Clarity on Architecture & Privacy

We believe in complete technical transparency. Here are honest answers regarding how your data is handled.

Your Secret Diary entries live strictly on your local device's browser sandbox (IndexedDB / localStorage). They are explicitly filtered out from Supabase database sync and excluded from Google Drive AppData backups. No remote server ever receives your diary text.
No, they are not encrypted by the application. Backups exported to Google Drive are structured plaintext JSON files stored in your private Google Drive AppData folder. We strongly encourage you not to store sensitive passwords or credentials in standard routine notes.
Never. The Google OAuth authorization requests only the restricted drive.appdata scope. This grants access exclusively to a hidden folder created specifically for UTTAKALA OS. The platform has zero access to your personal Google Docs, Sheets, Photos, or Drive storage.
User authentication credentials, daily routine completion states, course attendance logs, and task priorities are synchronized through Supabase managed tables, protected by user-level Row Level Security (RLS) policies.
